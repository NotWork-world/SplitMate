Eat together. Split only what you shared. Know exactly who owes whom.

SplitMate is a bill-splitting app for friends, flatmates, couples and travel groups. Its signature feature turns a restaurant bill into a stack of cards: swipe right to say who had each dish, left to come back to it later. Nothing is ever assigned to anyone automatically.

EXACT TO THE PAISA
• GST with CGST + SGST or IGST, tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive prices, never taxed twice
• Item discounts, bill discounts, coupons, service charge, packaging, delivery and tips
• Rounding and receipt totals: if the bill doesn't add up to the receipt, SplitMate tells you before anything is saved
• Every share adds up exactly to the total, and every amount is explained line by line

MORE THAN RESTAURANTS
• Rent, groceries, fuel, tickets, utilities and travel
• Split equally, by exact amounts, by percentage or by shares
• Several people can pay one bill; paying and eating are tracked separately
• Trips and home groups, plus a private personal space

WHO OWES WHOM, AND WHY
• Balances worked out only from finalised expenses and confirmed repayments
• Suggested payments that settle a group in as few transfers as possible
• Tap anyone to see every line behind your balance with them

REPAYMENTS YOU CAN TRUST
• Request money, record a payment, and settle partly or in full
• A payment counts only when the person who received it confirms it
• Disputes and rejections never change balances
• Open your own UPI app to pay a friend who shared their UPI ID

RECEIPT SCANNING (OPTIONAL)
• The receipt's text is read on your phone
• Review every line before it's added; unclear lines are highlighted
• Typing items by hand always works

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No ads and no tracking
• Friends don't need the app to be in your group
• Export your data or delete your account at any time
